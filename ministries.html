GROWING EVERY GENERATION

Our Ministries

Helping every generation grow in Christ, discover their gifts and serve.

Sunday School

Our weekly Sunday School provides Bible teaching, prayer, fellowship and activities for children and youth. We seek to help the next generation develop a strong foundation in God's Word and grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ.

Sundays · 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM

ACYM Youth

Adonai Christian Youth Movement encourages and equips the next generation to grow in faith, discover God-given talents and become active in God's work through Bible study, worship, fellowship and opportunities to serve.

VBS & Kids Camp

Each year our Vacation Bible School and Kids Camps help children learn God's Word in an enjoyable and engaging environment through Bible lessons, worship, games, creative programmes and fellowship.

Men's & Women's Meetings

Separate gatherings provide opportunities for believers to come together for prayer, fellowship, encouragement and spiritual growth, strengthening relationships and Christian living.

Intercessory Prayer

We come together online to seek God's guidance, blessing and intervention for individuals, families, our church, our community and the world.

Saturdays · 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Outreach Ministry

Sharing the love of Christ through evangelism and prayer, reaching people with the hope of the Gospel and serving our wider community.
