WHO WE ARE

About Adonai

Growing together. Serving together. Following Christ together.

Our Story

Adonai Pentecostal Church was established with a vision to encourage believers and families to grow together in Christ and to share the Gospel with the world. From the beginning, one of our key priorities has been to encourage children, youth and families to stand firmly in their faith and use their God-given talents for God's glory.

Our strength comes from the unity of our church families. We are passionate about reaching the next generation and creating an environment where children and emerging leaders can discover their talents, develop their character, grow in God's Word and become committed followers of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see revival in this generation and to raise a people who are deeply rooted in God's Word, spiritually mature and passionately committed to Jesus Christ. We desire to see hearts awakened, lives transformed and communities impacted through the power of the Gospel.

Our Mission

Our mission is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ with clarity, compassion and power. Through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, discipleship and fellowship, we seek to create an environment where people can encounter God, grow in faith and develop a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

What We Believe

Our beliefs are firmly rooted in the Word of God and centred on the grace of God.

The Bible

The Holy Bible is the inspired and authoritative Word of God.

The Trinity

One God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Salvation

Salvation is God's gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ.

Water Baptism

Baptism by immersion is a public declaration of faith and obedience to Christ.

Baptism in the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit empowers believers for spiritual growth, witness and service.

Christ's Return

We believe in the Rapture, the Second Coming of Christ, eternal judgment and eternal life.

A church for every generation.

Generations growing together, serving together and glorifying God together.
